# Ecological-Impact-Assessment
The overarching aim of this report is to identify which of the two proposed study sites (north and south) in Lochranza, Isle of Arran, would be optimal for developing as a means to achieve additional income via carbon credits. This was investigated by conducting a Phase 1 habitat survey and a baseline ecological assessment (EcIA). The taxa studied included bats, birds, invertebrates (aquatic, terrestrial and moths), mammals (non-bat) and plants. This data gave an overview of the species diversity and abundance on each site, as well as being indicative of the habitat and water quality. 

R packages required are:
library(patchwork)
library(ggplot2)
library(cowplot)
library(ggplot2)
library(tidyverse)
library(tidyr)
library(dplyr)

Data for this analysis was collected during a field trip to the Isle of Arran, Scotland, between 09/09/2025 and 12/09/2025.
